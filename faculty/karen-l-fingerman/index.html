RESEARCH & SCIENTIFIC INTERESTS

Explore the questions behind the work.

Studies social and emotional aging, including how relationships with family and friends change across adulthood and how daily social life relates to older adults' physical and cognitive health.

Scholarly publicationJan 9, 2025

Intergenerational Relationships and Family Support: Evidence for Health and Well-Being in Studies Supported by the National Institute on Aging

Merril Silverstein, Karen L Fingerman, J Jill Suitor

The Gerontologist

This review brings together research on relationships across generations and the support families provide to older adults. It examines connections between family ties, health, and well-being.
